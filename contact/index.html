good questions

Before you call

How do I book a driving lesson?

Call or text 416-457-5778. Mention your licence level, where you live and your road test date if you have one.

What if she doesn’t answer?

She’s often in the car with a student and can’t pick up. Leave a message with your name and area, or send a text.

When are lessons available?

Seven days a week, by appointment.

Do I need to get to her for lessons?

No. She drives to you, so your lesson starts where you are.

Which areas does she cover?

All of Hamilton, including Mount Hope, Hamilton Mountain, Downtown, East and West Hamilton, Westdale, Ancaster, Dundas, Stoney Creek, Binbrook and Glanbrook, plus Caledonia.
