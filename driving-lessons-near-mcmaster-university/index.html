where you’ll practise

Around campus

Slow, busy streets with highway ramps right next door.

  1. Main Street West

    Wide and busy along the edge of campus, past McMaster University and the McMaster University Medical Centre.

  2. King Street West in Westdale

    Shops, crosswalks and painted bike lanes through Westdale Village. Slow down and keep scanning.

  3. The Westdale side streets

    Quiet residential streets with lower speed limits, like Newton Avenue and Paisley Avenue South, plus the curved North and South Ovals. Good for a first lesson.

  4. Highway 403 at Main Street West

    Ramps to the 403 at Main Street West and Aberdeen Avenue, right beside campus. A short drive to highway practice.

  5. Cootes Drive

    The divided road to Dundas, which picks up speed as it leaves the city.

  6. Emerson Street

    A lower-speed street through Ainslie Wood, on the south side of campus.

good questions

Lessons in the McMaster area: questions

Do you give driving lessons near McMaster University?

Yes. She drives to you in Westdale or Ainslie Wood, and lessons can use the streets around campus.

I’m an international student. Can I take lessons?

Yes. If you have a licence from another country, how you get an Ontario licence depends on where it’s from, and the newcomer’s licence guide explains the options. Lessons help with whatever is new to you about driving here.

Is there construction around campus?

Some. Metrolinx is doing utility inspections for the Hamilton LRT along the McMaster to Eastgate route until the end of 2026, with flaggers and detours where needed.
